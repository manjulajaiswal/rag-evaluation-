BrightAI

BrightAI is an artificial intelligence company founded in 2020 by Sarah Kim and Michael Turner. The company is headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. BrightAI develops machine learning software for business applications.

In 2021, BrightAI introduced VisionPro, a computer vision platform designed to help manufacturers detect defects in products using automated image analysis.

In 2024, BrightAI launched AssistIQ, an AI assistant platform designed for customer support teams. AssistIQ can analyze customer conversations and help support agents generate responses.

BrightAI has offices in Chicago, Atlanta, and Toronto. Its computer vision research group is located in Atlanta and focuses on improving automated visual inspection systems.

VisionPro focuses on analyzing images from manufacturing processes, while AssistIQ focuses on customer support conversations. Both products use machine learning but are designed for different business applications.

BrightAI primarily serves manufacturing, retail, and customer service organizations.
