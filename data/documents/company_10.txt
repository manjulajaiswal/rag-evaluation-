PixelCore

PixelCore is a technology company founded in 2017 by James Carter and Rina Kapoor. The company is headquartered in Los Angeles, California. PixelCore develops software for media processing and artificial intelligence.

In 2020, PixelCore launched RenderFlow, a media processing platform that helps companies process and transform large collections of images and videos.

In 2023, the company introduced DetectAI, an image analysis platform designed to identify objects and patterns in visual data. DetectAI is used by organizations that need automated image classification and detection.

PixelCore operates offices in Los Angeles, Vancouver, and Tokyo. Its computer vision research team is based in Tokyo.

RenderFlow focuses on processing media files, while DetectAI focuses on extracting information from images. Both products work with visual data but perform different tasks.

PixelCore primarily serves media companies, advertising organizations, and retail businesses.
