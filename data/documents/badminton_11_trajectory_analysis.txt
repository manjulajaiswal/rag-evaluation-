Badminton Shuttle Trajectory Analysis

Shuttle trajectory analysis studies how the shuttlecock moves through space during a badminton rally. A trajectory can be represented using shuttlecock positions collected across consecutive video frames.

A trajectory begins with shuttlecock detections or tracking results. If some frames contain missing or noisy detections, trajectory processing can be used to repair or smooth the resulting path.

Trajectory information can be used to identify movement patterns, estimate landing positions, and analyze changes in shuttle direction. It can also provide useful information for detecting events such as hits.

Some badminton computer-vision systems focus specifically on trajectory reconstruction from broadcast video. These systems may combine shuttle tracking with court detection and player pose information.

Trajectory prediction is a related but different task. Tracking estimates where the shuttlecock is or was, while trajectory prediction attempts to estimate where it will move in future frames.

Reliable trajectory information can therefore serve as an intermediate representation for higher-level badminton analytics.
