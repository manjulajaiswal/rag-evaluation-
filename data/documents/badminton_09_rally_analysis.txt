Badminton Rally Analysis

A rally is a continuous sequence of play between the start of a point and the point-ending event. Automatic rally analysis attempts to identify rally boundaries and analyze what happens within each rally.

Video-based rally analysis can use player movement, shuttlecock tracking, hit events, and temporal information. A system may first identify when a rally starts and ends and then analyze the individual events occurring inside it.

Rally detection can be implemented using frame-level classifiers or temporal models. Some badminton computer-vision systems use pose and shuttle features together with sequence models to determine whether frames belong to active play.

Once rallies are identified, additional statistics can be calculated. These can include rally duration, number of shots, player movement, shuttle trajectory, and court positioning.

Rally analysis connects lower-level computer-vision tasks with higher-level sports analytics. Shuttle detection and player tracking provide visual information, while rally segmentation organizes that information into meaningful units of gameplay.
