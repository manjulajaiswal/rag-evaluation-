Badminton Player Detection

Player detection identifies the people appearing on the badminton court in each video frame. The objective is usually to locate each player using a bounding box or another spatial representation.

Object-detection models such as YOLO can be used for player detection in badminton videos. A detector processes individual frames and identifies objects belonging to the person class. The resulting bounding boxes can provide the approximate position and size of each player.

Player detection is an important first step for movement analysis because later tracking systems need detections from consecutive frames. The detected player regions can also be used as input for pose estimation.

A badminton-specific detection system may need to distinguish the two players on the court from spectators, officials, or people outside the playing area. Camera angle and occlusion can make this more difficult in broadcast footage.

Player detection should be distinguished from player tracking. Detection determines where players appear in individual frames, while tracking associates detections across frames so that the movement of each player can be followed over time.
