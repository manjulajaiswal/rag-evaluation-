Badminton Shot Classification

Shot classification attempts to identify the type of badminton shot performed during a hit. Examples of badminton shot categories can include different attacking, defensive, and placement-oriented strokes.

Computer-vision systems can use information about player pose and shuttlecock movement as features for shot classification. A pose representation describes how the player's body is positioned, while shuttle features provide information about the shuttle's location and movement.

Shot classification is generally performed after or alongside hit detection because the system first needs to identify the relevant moment in the video. A sequence of frames around the hit can then be analyzed to determine the shot type.

Machine-learning models can be trained using annotated badminton clips where each hit has an associated shot label.

Shot classification differs from hit detection. Hit detection answers the question of when a player hits the shuttlecock, while shot classification attempts to determine what type of shot was performed.

Accurate shot classification can support player statistics, rally analysis, coaching applications, and tactical analysis.
