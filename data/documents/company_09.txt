NeuralEdge

NeuralEdge is an artificial intelligence infrastructure company founded in 2019 by Thomas Wright and Meera Iyer. The company is headquartered in San Jose, California. NeuralEdge develops systems for deploying machine learning applications efficiently.

In 2021, NeuralEdge launched InferNet, a machine learning inference platform designed to serve predictive models at high throughput. InferNet is used by organizations that need to process large numbers of predictions.

In 2024, NeuralEdge introduced DataPulse, a monitoring system designed to track data quality and changes in production machine learning pipelines.

NeuralEdge maintains offices in San Jose, Austin, and London. Its inference research group is based in Austin and focuses on improving machine learning serving performance.

InferNet focuses on serving machine learning predictions, while DataPulse focuses on monitoring data quality. Both products support organizations operating machine learning systems in production.

NeuralEdge serves financial services, e-commerce, and technology companies.
