TerraCompute

TerraCompute is a computing technology company founded in 2016 by Robert Evans and Kavya Rao. The company is headquartered in Denver, Colorado. TerraCompute develops infrastructure software for organizations running large computational workloads.

In 2019, TerraCompute introduced Grid, a distributed computing platform designed to divide computational workloads across multiple machines. Grid is commonly used for scientific and engineering workloads.

In 2023, TerraCompute launched EdgeRun, a low-latency computing platform designed to run applications closer to users and connected devices. EdgeRun is used by companies that require rapid responses from distributed applications.

TerraCompute has offices in Denver, Dallas, and Stockholm. Its distributed systems research team operates from Stockholm.

Grid focuses on distributing computational workloads across machines, while EdgeRun focuses on reducing latency by placing computation closer to users and devices.

TerraCompute works with engineering, telecommunications, and technology companies.
