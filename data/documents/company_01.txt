NovaTech

NovaTech is a technology company founded in 2018 by Maya Chen and Daniel Brooks. The company is headquartered in Austin, Texas. NovaTech initially focused on building tools for cloud infrastructure.

In 2021, NovaTech introduced Atlas, a platform designed for distributed data processing. Atlas became an important part of NovaTech's data infrastructure offerings and was adopted by organizations managing large-scale data workloads.

In 2023, NovaTech launched Orion, a real-time machine learning inference platform. Orion was initially designed for financial services companies that needed to make predictions with low latency.

NovaTech operates engineering offices in Austin, Toronto, and Berlin. Its research team is primarily based in Toronto and focuses on improving machine learning systems.

Although Atlas and Orion are both platforms developed by NovaTech, they serve different purposes: Atlas focuses on distributed data processing, while Orion focuses on real-time machine learning inference.
