TrackNet for Badminton

TrackNet is a deep-learning approach designed for tracking small and fast-moving objects in sports videos. It became particularly relevant to badminton because the shuttlecock occupies only a small number of pixels and can move quickly between consecutive frames.

Instead of relying only on a single image, TrackNet uses multiple consecutive video frames as input and predicts the location of the shuttlecock. The prediction can be represented as a heatmap showing where the shuttlecock is likely to be located.

TrackNet-based systems can therefore provide shuttlecock coordinates for badminton videos. These coordinates can be connected across frames to produce a trajectory.

Later versions such as TrackNetV3 provide additional functionality for badminton shuttlecock tracking and trajectory processing. Open-source implementations of TrackNetV3 are used in badminton analysis pipelines for shuttle detection and tracking.

TrackNet is primarily concerned with the shuttlecock rather than the players. Player detection, player tracking, pose estimation, and court analysis are separate components that can be combined with TrackNet in a larger badminton video-analysis system.
